I’m Muzafar Ali Bhurt

A law student, legal researcher, and founder of MuzafarLex — a platform dedicated to analyzing global legal challenges.

This work explores international law, human rights, constitutional issues, and geopolitical developments through close reading and careful writing. The aim is to give readers clarity, knowledge, and a sharper way of thinking about a crowded legal landscape.

Muzafar breaks down global conflicts, international institutions, war crimes, and legal reforms, with the goal of building awareness, justice, and opportunity.

He is also Founder and Executive Director of CIVIS Alliance, shaped alongside his academic commitments. The September 2025 essay sets out the purpose behind that work.
